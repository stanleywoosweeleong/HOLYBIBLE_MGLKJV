STUDY PACK  (data/study.bin)  —  LICENSING AND ATTRIBUTION

This file is kept as a separate, optional download and is NOT merged with the
Mongolian Bible text. It combines two independently licensed works.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STRONG'S HEBREW AND GREEK DICTIONARIES

    A Concise Dictionary of the Words in the Hebrew Bible (1894)
    Dictionary of Greek Words, from Strong's Exhaustive Concordance (1890)
    by James Strong, S.T.D., LL.D.  —  public domain.

    Greek XML e-text version prepared in 2006 by Ulrik Petersen
    (http://ulrikp.org) from the ASCII e-text.

    Hebrew data assembled from several public-domain sources and corrected
    against the originals by David Instone-Brewer (www.2LetterLookup.com).
    Editing, corrections and Unicode transliterations by David Troidl.

    JSON edition: Copyright Open Scriptures, 2009 and 2010.  CC BY-SA.
    https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs

    Attribution is displayed in the app under Settings and must remain.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Extracted from the KJV SWORD module, version 3.1,
    "King James Version (1769) with Strongs Numbers and Morphology".
    Source: https://gitlab.com/crosswire-bible-society/kjv

    The module's configuration declares:   DistributionLicense=GPL

    Old Testament Strong's data keyed by the volunteers of
        The Bible Foundation (bf.org).

3. WHY THIS FILE IS SEPARATE

The GPL label on the KJV module is why this data is shipped as its own
optional file with this notice, rather than being merged into the Mongolian
Bible data. The Mongolian text is the copyright of the Mongolian Bible
translation team and is not affected by, and does not fall under, the
licence of this study pack.
